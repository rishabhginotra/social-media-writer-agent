# NovaPulse Cloud Technologies — Global Brand & Social Media Guidelines (v3.2)

> **Note:** NovaPulse Cloud Technologies is a fictional enterprise B2B SaaS company created for demonstration and evaluation purposes.

## 1. Company Overview & Mission
- **Company Name:** NovaPulse Cloud Technologies ("NovaPulse")
- **Flagship Product Suite:**
  - **PulseGrid™:** Autonomous multi-cloud workload orchestration and FinOps engine.
  - **PulseShield™:** Zero-trust runtime security and compliance automation for cloud-native Kubernetes clusters.
  - **PulseFlow™:** Real-time streaming telemetry and predictive incident prevention platform.
- **Mission Statement:** *"Empowering engineering and enterprise leaders to scale resilient cloud systems without operational friction."*

---

## 2. Core Brand Voice & Tone Principles
1. **Pragmatic & Evidence-Backed:** Every claim about performance, cost reduction, or reliability must cite concrete metrics, customer benchmarks, or architectural mechanisms.
2. **Human-Centric Technical Clarity:** Explain complex distributed-systems concepts clearly without buzzword soup.
3. **Constructive & Confident (Never Arrogant):** Highlight NovaPulse's architectural differentiation without disparaging competitors by name.
4. **Action-Oriented:** Every post must guide the reader toward a clear, valuable next step (e.g., reading an architecture blueprint, registering for a live lab, benchmarking their cluster).

## 4. Platform-Specific Formatting & Structural Rules

### LinkedIn (`linkedin`)
- **Character Length:** 450 to 1,600 characters (sweet spot: 700–1,200 characters).
- **Hook Rule:** First 2 lines must be under 140 characters combined and state a quantifiable insight, contrarian observation, or concrete engineering challenge.
- **Readability:** Use short paragraphs (1–2 sentences) and clean bullet points (`•` or `-`).
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5 relevant hashtags placed at the very end of the post.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** Include a specific, low-friction CTA aligned with the intended outcome.

### X / Twitter (`x_twitter`)
- **Single Post Length:** Maximum 280 characters.
- **Thread Format (`x_thread`):** 3 to 5 connected posts separated by `[Post 1/N]`, each under 280 characters.
- **Hashtags:** Maximum 2 hashtags per post.
- **Style:** Punchy, high-signal, zero filler words.

### Instagram (`instagram`)
- **Caption Length:** 250 to 900 characters + visual carousel slide breakdown (`[Slide 1] ... [Slide 5]`).
- **Hashtags:** 4 to 8 targeted hashtags at the bottom of the caption.
- **Accessibility:** Mandatory `[Alt-Text Description]` block for visual assets.

### YouTube Community (`youtube_community`)
- **Length:** 200 to 600 characters.
- **Engagement Element:** Must include an interactive poll question with 3–4 options (`[Poll Option A]`, `[Poll Option B]`, etc.) to maximize subscriber engagement.

## 5. Mandatory Compliance Rules & Prohibited Content

### 5.1 Prohibited Words & Phrases (Zero Tolerance)
Posts containing any of the following terms will fail automated brand compliance review:
- `"100% guaranteed"` or `"zero downtime guaranteed"` (violates legal SLA disclaimers)
- `"revolutionary magic"` or `"AI magic"` (violates pragmatic engineering voice)
- `"crush the competition"` or `"legacy dinosaurs"` (violates non-disparagement policy)
- `"unlimited free tier forever"` (inaccurate pricing claim)
- `"synergy"`, `"paradigm shift"`, `"game-changer"`, `"disruptive ninja"`, `"rockstar"` (banned corporate buzzwords)

### 5.2 Mandatory Brand Elements
1. **Metric Grounding:** Every post must include at least one concrete numeric metric or benchmark sourced from the NovaPulse CRM, Marketing Database, or Historic Social Performance Database.
2. **Brand Attribution:** Must reference either `NovaPulse` or one of its official products (`PulseGrid`, `PulseShield`, `PulseFlow`) or include `#NovaPulse` / `#BuildWithNovaPulse`.
3. **No Unredacted PII:** Customer contact names, private email addresses, phone numbers, or internal deal IDs must never appear in public social copy.
4. **Compliance Score Threshold:** Drafts must achieve a minimum Brand Compliance Score of **90/100** with **0 critical violations** before human-in-the-loop publishing approval.
